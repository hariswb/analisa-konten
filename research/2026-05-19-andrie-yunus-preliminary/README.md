## Core Story: Acid Attack

Andrie was attacked with acid (*disiram air keras*) in Central Jakarta (Salemba area). The perpetrators were **4 TNI soldiers** from **BAIS TNI** (Indonesian Military Intelligence Agency).

**Injuries:**
- 20% body burns
- 40% eye damage
- Required skin graft surgery
- Estimated recovery: 2 years
- Treated at RSCM hospital

**Case progression:** Initially handled by Polda Metro Jaya (police), then transferred to **Puspom TNI** (Army Military Police), tried in **Pengadilan Militer II-08 Jakarta**.

## Interesting Angles for Further Research

### 1. TNI vs Civilian Jurisdiction
The case's transfer from police (Polda Metro) to military court (Puspom TNI → Pengadilan Militer) is central. Andrie himself filed a **judicial review at the Constitutional Court (MK)** against the Military Court Law (UU Peradilan Militer). TAUD also reported the military judge to MA and KY for ethics violations. **Core question:** Should soldiers who attack civilians face civilian or military courts?

### 2. RUU TNI Connection
Andrie was attacked while protesting/interrupting the **RUU TNI debate at DPR**. The attack and the bill are politically intertwined. DPR held special sessions on the acid attack. This connects a broader legislative debate (expanding TNI roles) to a real incident of military violence against a civilian.

### 3. TAUD's "16 Perpetrators" Claim
Official narrative: **4 soldiers**. TAUD claims **16 people** with distinct roles: monitors, executors, operational support. Pre-trial hearing (*praperadilan*) starts **May 20, 2026** (tomorrow). Major discrepancy between official and independent investigation.

### 4. BAIS TNI Involvement
The involvement of **BAIS TNI (military intelligence)** elevates this from "rogue soldiers" to a potentially coordinated operation. This is significant for the broader narrative on military accountability.

### 5. Prabowo's Political Position
As Defense Minister (and former TNI commander), Prabowo publicly stated military courts can give heavier sentences. His position is politically charged given the concurrent RUU TNI debate and his presidential administration.

### 6. Source Framing Comparison
CNBC frames it economically/politically ("DPR held special session"), CNN uses "aktivis" language, while Kompas uses more neutral "wakil koordinator" framing. A multi-source framing comparison could reveal editorial bias.

### 7. Cross-channel Relevance
The story touches **Hukum** (legal proceedings), **Demokrasi** (civil rights), **Keamanan** (TNI/military), and **Legislasi** (RUU TNI). Not just a crime story — it spans multiple systemic issues.
